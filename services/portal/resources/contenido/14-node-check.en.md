## Borrador del texto

**H1:** Check your node

Enter your node ID (e.g. `!a1b2c3d4`) or name and we will evaluate your configuration to help optimize performance without saturating the mesh.

### What we evaluate

We analyze your node's broadcasts over the last 7 days:
- **Telemetry intervals:** Ensuring device metrics are not emitted too frequently.
- **Battery health & boot loops:** Identifying solar undervoltage cycles and repetitive resets.
- **Hop limits:** Ensuring hops are capped at 3–4 to prevent packet duplication storms.
