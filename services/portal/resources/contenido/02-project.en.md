**H1:** The Project

{PROJECT_NAME} is the gateway to the LoRa radio mesh network of Cádiz and the rest of Andalusia. It gathers what happens across the network in a single place: active nodes, channel load, troubled nodes, and how you can join.

### What is the mesh

A decentralized network of small radio devices—nodes—that exchange messages without requiring Internet or cellular coverage. Packets hop from node to node until reaching their destination. Each node belongs to a citizen who deployed it at home, in a vehicle, or at a high elevation point. Together, they cover the territory using the license-free 868 MHz ISM band with very low power.

### What you will find here

- **[MeshView]({MESHVIEW_URL}):** live mesh traffic, packet by packet, showing routes and who hears whom.
- **[PotatoMesh]({POTATOMESH_URL}):** interactive map and chat for public channels, with node details.
- **Provincial Map** (on the [home page](/)): active node counts and estimated channel saturation across each Andalusian province.
- **[Rankings](/rankings):** top spectrum consumers, nodes in danger, and top contributors.
- **[Alerts](/alertas):** automated anomaly detection with risk levels and affected scopes.
- **[Bots](/bots)** for Telegram and Discord delivering alerts straight to your community group.
- **[Public API](/api)** for developers wishing to integrate real-time mesh data.
- **Guides** to [configure your node](/configura-tu-nodo) and [connect your gateway](/conecta-tu-gateway).

### Purpose

The mesh belongs to everyone, and a single misconfigured node can degrade communication for the entire community. This project aims to:

- Inspect network health at a glance.
- Detect risks early: dying repeater batteries, reboot loops, channel congestion, or bad parameters.
- Provide clear setup instructions and actionable diagnostic tools.
- Give the regional community an open hub for documentation and monitoring.

### Cádiz, and all Andalusia

Born in Chipiona (Cádiz), the project embraces all eight Andalusian provinces to build regional resilience.

### Non-profit and open

This project is completely free, non-profit, and open-source. It does not sell equipment, install commercial trackers, or charge fees.
